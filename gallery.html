About Us

KHÅK is a contemporary art exhibition venue in Ålesund, with three departments:

  1. "Utstillingshallen": changing exhibitions of contemporary art
  2. ""Grafikrommet": sale of graphics from over 100 Norwegian artists
  3. "Kunstsalen": exhibitions of a selection from the Kjell Holm Foundation's art collection

The name KHÅK is pronounced "kåk", a colloquial term for a house, residence or home. It is composed of four initials; Kjell Holm, the man who owned the art collection represented in the art hall, and Ålesund Kunstforening, which is responsible for the daily operation of KHÅK.
